Art Mail Club

What is a Mail Club?

A creative monthly mail club is meant to bring color, inspiration, and hands on projects directly to your doorstep.
Each letter delivers a curated assortment of artistic goods including stickers, postcards, art prints, collage materials, and guided prompts to spark imagination and break up the ordinary mailbox routine.

Perfect for makers, scrapbookers, art enthusiasts, stationery collectors, and everyday creatives looking for tangible, curated art delivered monthly. These collectible elements invite you to journal, decorate, and enjoy a dedicated moment of artistic play every month.

About Us

Hi, I'm Laura! I'm a mom, a passionate collage artist, and a firm believer in the magic of hands on creativity.
I love combining textures, vintage papers, and bold colors to bring unique ideas to life and that's exactly why I started this site and club.
I want to build a space where I can share my art directly with you, spark your own creative journey, and deliver a little piece of hands on inspiration straight to your doorstep every month.

How To Subscribe

  1. Pick Your Plan: Choose between a flexible month-to-month plan or a discounted prepaid subscription.
  2. Enter Your Info: Fill in your shipping details at checkout so we know where to send your happy mail.
  3. Unbox & Create: Sit back and relax! Your curated art package ships on the 1st of every month.
