Learn More

Joining a snail mail art club is all about discovering new creative possibilities, but learning art jargon shouldn't feel like a barrier to entry.
We've put together this quick guide to common terms so you feel completely at home with every pack you unbox.
Understanding these basics will help you follow along with our monthly guided prompts, experiment confidently with different textures and techniques, and easily share your creations with our growing community of fellow makers.

Common Art Terms

Collage
An art technique where various materials-such as paper cutouts, photos, fabric, and found objects-are assembled and pasted together onto a surface to create a brand-new composition.
Ephemera
Collectible paper items originally meant to be short-lived, such as vintage ticket stubs, postage stamps, receipts, postcards, and newspaper clippings used to add history and texture to art projects.
Mixed Media
Artwork that combines more than one type of art material or technique in a single piece-such as layering watercolor paint, colored pencils, and paper collage together.
Art Print
A high-quality reproduction of an original piece of artwork, printed on premium paper so you can display, collect, or frame the design without needing the original painting.
Stationery
Paper products designed for writing and correspondence, including custom letterhead, decorative envelopes, postcards, and note cards.
