Who Were the Signers

Famous Signers

Some of the most well-known signers of the Declaration of Independence include:

Thomas Jefferson
Representing Virginia, he was the principal author of the document and later became the third President of the United States.
Benjamin Franklin
A delegate from Pennsylvania, he was the oldest signer at age 70, a renowned scientist, diplomat, and philosopher.
John Adams
Representing Massachusetts, he was a fierce advocate for independence in Congress and later served as the second U.S. President.
John Hancock
The President of the Continental Congress, he is famous for his large, bold signature right in the center of the document.
Samuel Adams
A passionate revolutionary leader and organizer from Massachusetts.
Roger Sherman
A delegate from Connecticut who uniquely went on to sign all four major founding documents of the United States.

Each of these signers made significant contributions to the founding of the United States.They are remembered for their courage and commitment to the cause of American independence.
